Privacy

gdzienasniadanie.pl is a hobby project. Browsing the map needs no account and stores nothing about you.

What we store if you register

  • your username and email address (used for login only);
  • a hashed password — we never see or store it in readable form;
  • your ratings, your saved places, and places you submitted.

We set a session cookie to keep you logged in and a CSRF cookie to protect forms. No analytics or advertising trackers.

If you pick a reference point or use your location, its coordinates are sent to our server with each search to order places by distance. We do not save them in the database, but they can appear in standard server logs.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything above, {% if contact_email %}email {{ contact_email }}{% else %}contact the author (contact address not configured yet){% endif %}; the data is removed on request.

The map library is loaded from unpkg.com and map tiles from OpenStreetMap; both see your IP address when the map loads.
