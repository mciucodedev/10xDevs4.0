Privacy

gdzienasniadanie.pl is a hobby project. Browsing the map needs no account and stores nothing about you.

What we store if you register

  • your username and email address (used for login only);
  • a hashed password — we never see or store it in readable form;
  • your ratings, your saved places, and places you submitted.

We use one session cookie to keep you logged in. No analytics or advertising trackers. Your location is used in your browser only, to order places, and is not sent to be stored.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything above, contact the author at the address on the project page; the data is removed on request.

Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address when tiles load.
